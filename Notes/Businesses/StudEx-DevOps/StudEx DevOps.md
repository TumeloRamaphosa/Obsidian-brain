# StudEx DevOps

> The technology backbone powering the StudEx ecosystem — and a sellable service.

## Overview

StudEx DevOps is the technology and development arm of [[StudEx Group]]. It builds the internal tools, AI systems, and infrastructure that power all StudEx businesses — AND offers these capabilities as services to external clients.

## Dual Mission

### 1. Internal — Power StudEx
- Build and maintain the Obsidian Second Brain system
- AI-powered business intelligence and automation
- Data pipelines for [[StudEx Meat]] (genetics tracking, supply chain)
- Trading systems for [[StudEx Global Markets]] (market data, logistics)
- DevOps infrastructure across all entities

### 2. External — Sell the Stack
- Offer AI/DevOps consulting to other businesses
- Productize internal tools (knowledge management, automation)
- Build custom solutions for agriculture, trade, and commodity sectors
