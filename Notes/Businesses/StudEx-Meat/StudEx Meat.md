# StudEx Meat

> From stud genetics to premium protein on the plate.

## Overview

StudEx Meat is the agricultural and livestock arm of [[StudEx Group]]. It focuses on premium genetics breeding programs and the full meat production value chain.

## Value Chain

```
Genetics → Breeding → Rearing → Processing → Distribution → Consumer
   │           │          │          │             │            │
   ▼           ▼          ▼          ▼             ▼            ▼
 Pedigree   Stud       Feedlot   Abattoir     Cold chain    Retail/
 semen &    services   & farm    & cutting    & logistics   HoReCa
 embryos    & AI       mgmt      plants                    & export
```

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams
1. **Breeding Stock Sales** — Selling pedigree animals to other breeders
2. **Genetics (Semen & Embryos)** — High-value genetics sold domestically and internationally
3. **Premium Meat Products** — Branded, traceable, premium cuts
4. **Stud Services** — AI (artificial insemination) and breeding consultation
5. **Contract Farming** — Partnership programs with emerging farmers

### Target Markets
- Premium domestic retail (butcheries, restaurants)
- Export markets (Middle East, Asia, Europe)
- Breeding stock for African continent
- Genetics export globally

## Competitive Advantages

- **Genetics-first approach** — Start with the best DNA
- **Full traceability** — From sire to steak
- **Premium brand positioning** — Not commodity meat
- **Vertical integration** — Own the full chain
- **Export via [[StudEx Global Markets]]** — Built-in trade arm
