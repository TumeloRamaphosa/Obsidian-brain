# StudEx Global Markets

> Taking premium African products to the world.

## Overview

StudEx Global Markets is the international trade and commodity arm of [[StudEx Group]]. It connects African producers — starting with [[StudEx Meat]] — to global demand.

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams
1. **Trade Commissions** — Facilitating buyer-seller connections
2. **Commodity Brokerage** — Agricultural commodity trading
3. **Logistics & Freight** — End-to-end export logistics management
4. **Market Intelligence** — Data and insights for producers and buyers
5. **Trade Finance Facilitation** — Connecting exporters with finance

### Core Activities
```
African Producers ──→ Sourcing ──→ Quality Control ──→ Logistics ──→ Global Buyers
                         │              │                 │              │
                         ▼              ▼                 ▼              ▼
                     Grading &     Certification     Cold chain      Contracts &
                     selection     & compliance      & shipping      settlement
```

## Target Markets

### Export Destinations
| Region | Products | Opportunity |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Middle East (UAE, Saudi) | Premium beef, lamb | High demand, premium pricing |
| Asia (China, Vietnam) | Beef, offal, hides | Volume + emerging premiums |
| Europe (UK, Netherlands) | Certified organic/grass-fed | Niche premium |
| Africa (SADC, EAC) | Live animals, genetics, meat | Regional trade |

### Products to Trade
- Premium meat (via [[StudEx Meat]])
- Live breeding stock and genetics
- Agricultural commodities (grains, oilseeds)
- Processed agricultural products
- Hides and by-products

## Competitive Advantages

- **Integrated supply** via [[StudEx Meat]] — guaranteed quality source
- **African market knowledge** — on-the-ground intelligence
- **Multi-commodity** — not dependent on single product
- **Tech-enabled** — AI for market analysis and trade matching
- **Compliance expertise** — navigate complex export regulations
