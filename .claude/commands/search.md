Deep search the entire Obsidian vault for information related to a query.

## Steps

1. **Keyword search** — Grep for exact and related terms across all .md files
2. **Tag search** — Find notes with relevant tags
3. **Link search** — Follow wikilinks from matching notes to discover connected knowledge
4. **Business context** — Check if results relate to [[StudEx Group]], [[StudEx Meat]], or [[StudEx Global Markets]]
5. **Synthesize** — Combine findings into a coherent answer

## Output
- Direct answer to the query
- List of all relevant notes found (as [[wikilinks]])
- Related topics to explore
- Gaps — what the vault doesn't know yet about this topic
